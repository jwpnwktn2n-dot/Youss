How automatic scanning works

When the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, the included scheduled workflow checks AMMC announcements and public Moroccan-market news feeds several times a day and publishes a fresh JSON feed. Opening this app fetches that feed. News is not scanned while the app is closed unless the scheduled workflow has actually been deployed and enabled.

Bundled news is a dated snapshot. Automatic scanning is inactive until you deploy the workflow. No fake “live” status.

Keep your data safe

Your information stays in this browser's local storage. It is not synced to banks or transmitted to us. Clearing browser data or changing phones may erase it, so export a backup regularly.

Language & synchronization

Tap Sync to fetch the latest published snapshot. Personal entries are saved on this device, not cloud-synced.

Data & privacy

Your salary, transactions, and savings are stored locally in this browser. The Casablanca market files are separate. This is a research app, not a brokerage or financial advice service.

MyMoney is a personal planning tool, not a bank or investment platform. Never enter bank passwords or account credentials here.
